Questions people ask me

Is the price really one-time?

Yes. The website plans are a one-time payment. The only monthly plan on this page is ServerFall Monitoring ($19/month), and it's optional. You can cancel it anytime.

What do I need to send you?

Your business name, what you do, the area you serve, your phone and email. Photos and a logo help, but if you don't have them, I'll help you with that too.

I don't have a domain name (like mybusiness.com). Is that a problem?

Not at all. I'll help you choose and set up your domain. The domain itself is paid to the registrar (usually around $12–20 per year) and it's registered in your name, so it's always yours.

How long does it take?

It depends on the plan and on how fast you send me your info. A one-page website is the quickest. After our first talk, I'll give you a clear date.

What if I don't like it?

Every website plan comes with unlimited revisions. I'll keep adjusting it until you're happy with it.

Is it safe to pay on your website?

Yes. Payments are processed by Stripe, one of the most trusted payment companies in the world. Your card details go straight to Stripe — I never see them.

You're not in the US. Is that okay?

Yes. I already work with clients in the United States and Europe. We talk by email, chat, WhatsApp or video call, at a time that works for you.
