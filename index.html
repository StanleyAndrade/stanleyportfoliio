Questions people ask me

Is the price really one-time?

Yes. The website plans and the Google Business Profile setup are a one-time payment. The only monthly plan on this page is ServerFall Monitoring ($19/month), and it's optional. You can cancel it anytime.

How much does an app or custom software cost?

It depends on what it needs to do. After a free call I send you a clear, fixed quote, so you know the full price before we start. No surprise charges.

Do I get one app for both Android and iPhone?

Yes. I build one app that works on both and help you publish it on Google Play and the App Store. The store accounts (Google Play and Apple Developer) are created in your name, so the app is always yours.

What do I need to send you?

For an app or system, a simple description of your idea or of what you do by hand today is enough. I ask the right questions. For a website: your business name, what you do, the area you serve, your phone and email. Photos and a logo help, but I can help with that too.

I don't have a domain name (like mybusiness.com). Is that a problem?

Not at all. I'll help you choose and set up your domain. The domain itself is paid to the registrar (usually around $12–20 per year) and it's registered in your name, so it's always yours.

How long does it take?

It depends on the project and on how fast you send me your info. A one-page website is the quickest; apps and systems take longer. After our first talk, I'll give you a clear date.

What if I don't like it?

Every website plan comes with unlimited revisions. For apps and software, you review it along the way and I adjust it before launch, until you're happy with it.

Is it safe to pay on your website?

Yes. Payments are processed by Stripe, one of the most trusted payment companies in the world. Your card details go straight to Stripe — I never see them.

You're not in the US. Is that okay?

Yes. I already work with clients in the United States and Brazil. We talk by email, chat, WhatsApp or video call, at a time that works for you.
