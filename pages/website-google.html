Questions people ask me

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay once and the website is yours. The only yearly cost is your domain name (like yourbusiness.com), usually around $12–20 per year, paid to the registrar and registered in your name.

What is a Google Business Profile?

It's the free listing that shows your business on Google Search and Google Maps, with your hours, phone, address, photos and reviews. It's often the first thing people see before they call you.

I already have a website. Can I get just the Google profile?

Yes. The Google Business Profile setup alone is $199, one-time. It also comes included in the Standard and Business website plans.

What do I need to send you?

Your business name, what you do, the area you serve, your phone and email. Photos and a logo help, but I can help with that too.

How long does it take?

It depends on the plan and on how fast you send me your info. After our first talk, I'll give you a clear date.

What if I don't like it?

Every website plan comes with unlimited revisions. I adjust it until you love it.

Is it safe to pay on your website?

Yes. Payments are processed by Stripe, one of the most trusted payment companies in the world. Your card details go straight to Stripe — I never see them.

You're not in the US. Is that okay?

Yes. I already work with clients in the United States and Brazil. We talk by email, chat, WhatsApp or video call, at a time that works for you.
